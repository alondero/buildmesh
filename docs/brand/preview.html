Buildmesh — logo & wordmark

Three alternatives built on the existing identity: the M formed from a mesh of connected nodes, the Electric Cyan #00d4ff → emerald gradient, and the app's own type stack. Each group ships a mark, a dark lockup and a light lockup.

A

Lattice

Heritage · refined

The current mark, sharpened. Geometry is rebuilt on a clean grid with round joins, the front M gets a subtle brand bloom, and the translucent rear weave is kept but tuned so it reads as deliberate depth rather than a stray second stroke. Lowest-risk option — it will feel like the same logo, better made.

B

Nexus

Product · app icon

The mesh becomes explicit: five nodes wired into an M inside a rounded plate. The gradient runs cyan → violet → emerald, pulling the app's agent-violet into the brand. Self-contained on its own dark plate, so it works as an app icon, avatar and favicon without needing a light/dark rethink. The wordmark takes the same tri-tone gradient.

C

Prism

Stamp · icon-first

Flat and structural. Two solid columns joined by an inner V, capped with beams at the peaks so the mark reads as a built frame rather than a drawn line. No gradients, no glow, two solid hues split hard at the valley — it survives 16px, embroidery and single-colour print. The wordmark switches to the app's mono stack for a terminal-native feel, over a gradient mesh rail.
